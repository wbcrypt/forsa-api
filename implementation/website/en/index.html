Simple by design.

From application to active member in days.

1

Membership Request

Submit a free Membership Request in minutes — no documents required at this stage.

2

Become a Bronze Member

Once approved, you receive your FORSA ID and FORSA Digital Pass, and activate your account.

3

Apply for Facilitation

Need help with your university fees? Complete an interview with our AI — it recommends, it never decides.

4

Decision & Activation

The review committee makes the final decision. If approved for Silver or Gold, you meet FORSA to activate your plan.
